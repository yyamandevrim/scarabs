## One generated look

All six generated scenes share an overcast early dawn, Finnish-looking spruce/birch forest, low granite ridges, cool mineral blue-grey shadows and muted natural greens. Diffuse silver sky; no orange firelight, golden sunset or fantasy fog. Natural contrast, subtle fine 35 mm film grain, realistic vegetation, restrained 35 mm full-frame lens character and deep enough focus to read the land. Slow, mechanically plausible camera moves; no dramatic swoops. 16:9 throughout. Reuse the approved first frame's style reference for later shots if available. It establishes a fictional landscape, never a verified site or a real Finnish data output.

The palette echoes the interface; it does not repaint screenshots. Add **Illustration** in the edit for every generated frame; never ask the model to render captions, logos or UI. All generated material is context or a clearly labelled concept. Nothing generated may occupy a camera tile, evidence player, coverage map or product-result inset.

## Capture preparation

Follow `DEMO.md` A–B privately before recording. Use its sustainable stage configuration, English Ground and Placement, and the unchanged Turkish Station. Ground starts before Station. Let legal simulated packet slots run; archive events only as the runbook instructs, preserving the duty ledger. Record the browser content area without address bars, login, terminals, credentials, personal paths or notifications. Keep original source/provenance labels visible. Use a separate editor-added ACORN text treatment over existing header artwork if needed; do not change code.

SCREEN means the actual app, never a recreated UI. Collect longer continuous takes first, then trim to the listed window. Do not accelerate a detection or delivery into a false latency claim. A cut between already-prepared states is acceptable with the recorded-demo and simulated-transport labels retained. Capture screenshots from these same takes for the deck. If a state fails, use a labelled earlier successful capture or omit it; never fabricate a result.
