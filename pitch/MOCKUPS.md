**One REAL shot (20, 3 s), one required original photograph, zero live-action filming.** Shoot the powered assembly at the venue: real UNO Q, existing hub/wiring, two connected USB cameras, KY-026 and on-board LED visible together, neutral current state. Use a sharp landscape original at least 3840×2160, diffuse room/window light, no flash, no private screen or badge. A second detail still is optional only if labels are unreadable; it adds no film shot. No team photo or sensor-stimulus filming is required.

Caption **Actual prototype · still photograph** and retain actual capture date/setup in the asset log. A photo proves assembly exists, not current operation, smoke sensitivity or LED response. Therefore end voice is “This is the prototype we built,” not “running.” Zero REAL shots would remove the judges' only direct evidence of the hardware; a render cannot supply that.
