# ACORN hardware and brand capture manifest

Captured 2026-11-15 from commit **0027611 — working tree with uncommitted changes**. The supplied OBJ/MTL and canonical SVG files were read directly. No tracked source was edited, committed, pushed or deployed. No product app, API, external service, real display or port was used. Hardware source units are metres, with rigid Z-up to Y-up rotation and recentering only. Source checksums are in `hardware-work/source-sha256.json`; geometry provenance is in `hardware-work/geometry-provenance.json`.

## Deliverables

**40 video files representing 11 takes / framing variants; 131 PNG stills.** Take duration: **92 s** (includes square/portrait hero takes). Total duration across all codec/resolution exports: **320 s**. Hardware: 7 takes, 74 s; brand: 4 takes, 18 s. Landscape exports are 2880×1620 plus 1920×1080 in both H.264 and alpha ProRes. Hardware hero square is 1620×1620 and portrait 1080×1920; brand square is 1620×1620.

Every MP4 is H.264/yuv420p, constant 30 fps, CRF 15 (hardware) or 14 (brand), no audio and faststart. Every transparent video is ProRes 4444 MOV with alpha. Native transparent PNGs are clean product/artwork cutouts. Hardware dark versions include a soft stage contact shadow; transparent versions omit the stage/shadow. Source frame times are deterministic, independent WebGL renders. All turntables use 360 different frames and wrap one-frame angular spacing across the loop boundary. Push/orbit/top clips include 1 s start/end holds; assembly reveal includes deliberate holds between gentle fades. Logo clips use restrained SVG fade/scale, a brief blank opening and a 1 s final hold. Short 3 s/6 s logo clips and seamless turntable handles follow the explicit surface request.

## Fidelity and limitations

- The OBJ is intact but modestly tessellated (7,994 vertices, 16,028 triangles). Circular silhouettes retain the source facets. Optical faces are plain flat source discs: no added lenses, rings, sensors, body logo, print, ropes or electronics. This is a CAD visualization, not a photograph or a validated production assembly.
- Material categories and neutral satin finish follow the hardware README / Overview viewer. The supplied MTL alone defines one pale blue diffuse material, without a physical plastic/optic specification. Lighting, smooth creased normals and vertex AO affect appearance only; no source positions or proportions are changed.
- The 225 OBJ names are surface fragments. The part reveal uses only the six documented assemblies at their real assembled locations; no exploded mechanism or internal electronics is fabricated.
- Exact top-down views faithfully show the three-pod layout at about 120°. The vertical optical faces are edge-on from above; individual face stills show each disc frontally.
- All six canonical logos are unaltered SVGs, including original colors and vector caption lettering. White artwork on white is visually blank and black artwork on dark has low contrast: those requested combinations are intentionally preserved, with readable opposite-color and transparent alternatives.
- `assets/icon.svg` is a legacy teal **J** icon, not an ACORN logo. Its 9 background/size reference stills are clearly named `legacy-icon-j` and kept separate from the six canonical logos.
- The model itself has no broken or missing geometry found in review; its flat optical faces and lack of electronics are source limitations. No product UI was part of this surface.

## Verification

Every MP4 and MOV was ffprobed, fully decoded, checked for exact dimensions/frame rate/duration and no audio, and visually reviewed at start/middle/end. Every still was decoded and reviewed. Alpha channels were checked on transparent stills and MOV samples. `verification.json`, `hardware-work/cadence.json`, `hardware-work/qa/` and `brand-work/qa-results.json` retain evidence. The portrait hero was reframed after a tight initial crop; only the corrected export is delivered. The assembly reveal was retaken with continuous fades rather than stepped stills. All task browser/encoder processes exited; no server was started.

`contact-sheet.png` tiles one representative image from **every delivered clip file and still**, including resolution/codec variants. Checkers show transparency; they are contact-sheet presentation only. `hardware-work/` and `brand-work/` contain reproducible scripts, original render frames, logs and QA support files, not additional deliverable takes.
