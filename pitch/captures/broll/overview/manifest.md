# Overview b-roll capture manifest

Captured 2026-11-15. Deliverables are `video/*.mp4`, `stills/*.png`, `contact-sheet.png`, and this manifest. Raw frames, diagnostics and rejected experiments are supporting material.

Source: http://127.0.0.1:8301/ from `/Users/yamandevrim/.t3/projects/junction/placement`; commit `0027611` — working tree with uncommitted changes.

Headless Playwright Chromium, Apple Metal WebGL. Only Overview `/` captured; the map appears only as its embedded, responsive preview. English UI, local media and local assets. All actions use real controls and scrolling. No DOM content changes, injected reports, substituted media, cursor, browser chrome, OS capture, audio, external services or uploads.

Landscape: 1440×810 CSS, DPR 2, native 2880×1620; each has a 1920×1080 export. Phone: 393×852 CSS, DPR 3, native 1179×2556; H.264 adds one black pixel column at right to 1180×2556. PNGs remain 1179×2556. Detail crops are native 2x; full-page images preserve DPR.

Video: H.264, CRF 14, yuv420p, limited range, CFR 30 fps, faststart, no audio. The native Chromium compositor stream (JPEG 95) uses its original frame timestamps and the real recording clock; unchanged screens emit no new frames. No optical flow or synthetic intermediate motion. Holds naturally repeat unchanged pixels. App evidence media is native 640×280, 15 fps, 6.2 s; its original cadence is preserved. Each take includes at least one-second handles.

The radio and phone sequences are the Overview’s built-in explanatory demo and its existing fixed event fixture; they are not live hardware or radio measurements. Evidence is collected only through the app’s Wi-Fi demo control.

CAD proof: `#model-stage.ready`, poster hidden, live `window.acornModel.stats` reports rendered geometry/calls, Apple M5 Metal renderer; camera selections/drag change its rendered pixels. See `capture.log`.
