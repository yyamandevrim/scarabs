- Capture: own headless Chromium only, no user display/foreground input. - `presentation.js` is a formatting helper (numbers, bearings, percentages and transport labels); it exposes no user-facing presentation mode, so there is no mode to film. Only station 1 exists in this isolated packet stream.

## Product observations and limits

1. Startup status can label warming cameras “lost”; the displayed camera state remains the last status packet until a later legal transmission reports recovery. The red startup sectors in first-status/arrival clips are real.
2. After collection, the footer message can still say “Enable the Wi-Fi demo to view it here” while that demo is already on.
3. Phone layout is usable with vertical scrolling; compass, queue and evidence do not share one portrait viewport. Landscape remains the primary operator view.
