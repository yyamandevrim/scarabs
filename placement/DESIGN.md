# ACORN interface design

The planner answers “which launch point sees more forest?” The station answers “what do the cameras show, and which suspicion needs a decision?” Give each answer a single home.

## Visual system

Dark mineral surfaces: canvas #090e13, panel #0f161d, grouped controls #151e27, selected surface #1c2733. Primary text #e3e9ee, secondary #a7b4bf, quiet text #8697a5. Soft blue #8fc3e3 is the sole interaction accent, used for focus and links. Main actions are neutral. Amber #f2a93b means suspected smoke; red #f0594e means unavailable or failed. The local flame sensor is labelled explicitly; its physical LED colours are reported as data, not adopted as interface decoration.

IBM Plex Sans, locally vendored under the SIL Open Font License (static/fonts/OFL-IBM-Plex-Sans.txt and OFL-IBM-Plex-Mono.txt). Proportional labels in sentence case; 12–14 px supporting text, 15–20 px headings, 32 px result figures, 52 px landing headline. IBM Plex Mono is reserved for coordinates, identifiers and dense numeric tables. Tabular figures throughout. No clock in the planner, tracked capitals or ornamental engine indicators. Small simulation labels stay attached to headline figures and the verdict so isolated screenshots retain their meaning.

## Planner

At 1440 px, map width is 1020 px and the only panel is 420 px. Its Compare tab owns the two site figures, one verdict, camera-pair choice and heading. The active site is underlined and its marker is white; no competing accent. Heading sensitivity expands on demand. Position, angles and reference configuration live in a disclosure. The Breakdown tab owns accounting and expert table; Data owns provenance and assumptions. The three-camera reference is available in the table and settings, not repeated among achievable pairs. A footer states the geometric simulation limit once. During recalculation, old results are explicitly marked as previous settings.

The map is the signature: quiet local terrain beneath coloured visibility cells and readable camera sectors. The backend map palette and cached simulation masks remain unchanged. A bounded local canvas cache remaps visible forest to bright mint (#5aefb0) and lowers water opacity; display tokens also drive the legends. No areas or category masks change. Brightness separates visible targets from water independently of hue. The basemap retains its original land-cover hues under a muted dark filter. A fully opaque rectangle is derived from the raster alpha mask; minimum zoom and pan bounds prevent exposure of warped raster edges. Controls form one corner cluster. The selected footprint is solid; the other site uses faint dashed sectors without a second colour raster, so overlapping targets cannot hide each other. Legend occupies its own bottom strip. On phones the map precedes the comparison and controls; independent scrolling is desktop only.

The landing page gives the real product preview the full content width. A short explanation and action precede it; method, real data sources and measured station context follow. No invented outcomes or metric widgets.

## Station

At desktop width, two larger complete feed frames stack beside their measurement metadata, with a separately scrolling 400 px review queue. The newest three records and oldest unanswered remain visible; older records expand behind their actual count. Chronological jump controls and distinct newest/oldest labels help triage. An open or focused row stays visible through polling and collapse. The pending total appears only in the queue heading. A single event opens initially; other rows show camera and time. Expanding another event is explicit and never restarts or replaces a playing clip during polling. Decisions move into history; correction remains available. Only actual data drives timing, device identity and sensor/LED state. Flame sensing is a separate local-module status, never confirmation of a camera event. Runtime requires only local fonts, CSS, JS, imagery and JSON, without animation or heavy libraries.

## Shared product shell and Ground

All three applications serve the same token stylesheet and local IBM Plex weights (SIL OFL). The 48 px desktop shell uses the compact supplied wordmark, a proportional module label, a quiet connection or navigation context, and the same 32 px controls. Phone headers wrap to preserve readable status and touch targets. Applications stay independently runnable; copy tokens to all three static directories together.

Ground gives the azimuth compass the calm canvas and the received-alert queue a 400 px panel, matching the placement division of attention. Five-degree ticks, cardinal directions and restrained camera sectors support the selected approximate bearing. The short white arrow is explicitly labelled as configured module heading. There are no distance rings or claims of measured position. The selected alert stays first as new packets arrive, keeping its compass and evidence context steady. Alert status has one chip; packet IDs and box dimensions sit behind a disclosure. When the Wi-Fi demo is enabled on desktop, the selected alert's collected clip appears beside the compass, with the snapshot and live feeds available on demand. Live frame requests run only while their disclosure is open. Media is always labelled Wi-Fi-only; the packet link carries neither imagery nor an analysis profile.

Station retains large complete frames, with effective profile parameters next to the measurements, a mounted inactive-camera tile and a static swatch of the LED state actually returned by the API. Simulated MCU data carries an explicit badge. Labelled event disclosures replace glyphs. Polling preserves queue targets while pointer or focus is inside; leaving applies deferred arrivals and opens the next pending record after a decision. No animated LED, fabricated telemetry or profile switch is added.
