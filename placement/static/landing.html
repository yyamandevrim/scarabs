Operator station

Measured

Runs on the balloon module: analyses two camera inputs locally, raises a smoke suspicion when three of five analyses agree, and asks an operator to confirm or reject each one. Its panel is local to the device and is not published on this website.

Forest the cameras miss has two causes

Outside the field of view, shown grey: forest beyond the two connected cameras’ view. Occluded in the field of view, shown rust: forest the cameras face but terrain or canopy hides along the sightline. The blind total is always the sum of the two.

What the numbers do not say

The figures are geometric visibility, not a smoke-detection range. The model treats canopy as opaque, uses estimated stand heights and a fixed smoke-target height, and leaves out buildings, weather, tether motion and camera calibration. Access, shadow, site use and flight permissions still need checks on site.

Smoke suspicions are not confirmed fires: the station only flags them, and an operator decides.
