OBSERVE → REVIEW → DECIDE

Local by design.
Human in the loop.

The station can analyse camera images without an internet uplink. This public website runs the separate placement simulator; it never exposes station feeds.

CAPTURE

Three viewpoints. Two inputs.

Left −70°, centre 0°, right +70°. Any two cameras can be active. The third remains mounted and disconnected.

DETECT

Suspicion needs persistence.

The on-device model opens a smoke-suspicion event when overlapping detections appear in at least 3 of the last 5 analyses on the same camera. The default box overlap threshold is IoU 0.25.

REVIEW

An operator makes the call.

A local panel shows evidence and lets an operator confirm or reject a suspicion. Decisions persist locally. A suspicion is not a verified fire.

PLACE BEFORE YOU LAUNCH

Where you look matters
as much as how high.

Click a land location in the Helsinki capital region. The backend traces terrain and canopy sightlines, compares all camera pairs, and separates forest outside the active field of view from forest occluded inside it.

Real Finnish elevation, land-cover and forest rasters underpin a geometric simulation. A 12 km range circle is a modelling boundary, not an optical detection range.

MEASURED · MAC ONLY

57.74 ms mean inference

Apple M5 Mac, CPU execution, 640 px model input, 54 samples from a recorded wildfire clip. Three warm-ups excluded. This is not Arduino UNO Q performance.

Recorded-clip demo measurements; selected clips do not establish field detection or false-alarm rates. UNO Q hardware is unverified.

SIMULATED · GEOMETRY

Visibility, not detection.

Every hectare figure is simulated. Opaque canopy, estimated stand heights, a 2019 DEM and a fixed smoke-target height simplify reality. Building heights, weather, tether motion and optical calibration are absent.

No claims of fires prevented, carbon saved or biodiversity impact. Launch access, airspace and suitability require field checks.
