Where you point the cameras changes the result.

The coastal preset points mostly over water. The inland preset looks over forest. Change position, heading, height or camera pair to see how much of that forest enters the simulated view.

The map separates forest outside the active field of view from forest hidden by terrain or canopy inside it. A 12 km model boundary is not an optical smoke-detection range.

What this prototype can tell you

Real NLS elevation, Syke land cover and Luke forest data support the geometric calculation. Opaque canopy, fixed smoke-target height and estimated stand heights simplify the scene. Buildings, weather, tether motion and camera calibration are not modelled.

The separate local station analyses camera footage and asks an operator to review smoke suspicions. This website demonstrates placement; it does not expose station feeds.

Access, shadow, site use and flight permissions require field checks. No field deployment or impact claims.
