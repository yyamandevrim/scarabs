Which launch point lets two balloon cameras see the most forest?

The launch point map compares candidate sites in the Helsinki capital region. The balloon module carries three cameras, two connected at a time. For each site it simulates how many hectares of forest fall inside the connected cameras’ view, and which pair is worth connecting.

Open the launch point map

Runs offline on NLS, Syke and Luke data. Every hectare is simulated.

Forest the cameras miss has two causes

Outside the field of view, shown grey: forest beyond the two connected cameras’ view. Occluded in the field of view, shown rust: forest the cameras face but terrain or canopy hides along the sightline. The blind total is always the sum of the two.

What the numbers do not say

The figures are geometric visibility, not a smoke-detection range. The model treats canopy as opaque, uses estimated stand heights and a fixed smoke-target height, and leaves out buildings, weather, tether motion and camera calibration. Access, shadow, site use and flight permissions still need checks on site.

A separate station runs on the balloon module: it analyses camera footage locally and asks an operator to confirm or reject each smoke suspicion. This website shows placement only and does not expose station feeds.
