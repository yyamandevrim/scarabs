# ACORN launch point map

## Register
product

## Users
A hackathon jury comparing launch points in a two-minute demonstration, and operators exploring the Helsinki region offline.

## Product Purpose
Compare simulated forest visibility from a tethered balloon with three mounted cameras and two active inputs. Show which candidate and camera pair yield more visible forest under the chosen geometry.

## Brand Personality
Precise, calm, candid. The product is ACORN (Aerial Canopy Observation and Reporting Node); identity comes only from brand.json, the logo is still a placeholder, and Scarabs is the team.

## Anti-references
Generic dashboard cards, marketing slogans, decorative badges, illustrative product substitutes, tiny grey figures and ambiguous zeros.

## Design Principles
Show the real map. Put the decision beside its evidence. Keep simulation limits visible. Separate uncomputed values from measured zero. Use progressive disclosure for secondary controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
English default and complete Turkish toggle. System fonts, minimum 12px, tabular figures, keyboard focus, reduced motion, distinguishable map categories with a textual legend.
