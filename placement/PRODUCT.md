# ACORN launch point map

## Register
product

## Users
Investors comparing the product's launch-point value in a short demonstration, and operators exploring the Helsinki region offline.

## Product Purpose
Compare simulated forest visibility from a tethered balloon with three simultaneously operating cameras on a triangular housing. Each camera is modelled as a 120° horizontal sector by product decision, at yaw 0°/120°/240°, closing the full 360°. Compare sites, heights and visible forest; heading only rotates sector seams.

## Brand Personality
Precise, calm, clear. ACORN expands to Aerial Canopy Observation and Reporting Node. Identity and supplied vector artwork come from brand.json; Scarabs is the team.

## Anti-references
Generic dashboard cards, decorative badges, illustrative product substitutes, tiny grey figures and ambiguous zeros.

## Design Principles
Show the real map. Put site totals and a single verdict beside the evidence. Keep simulated labels on hectare figures. Height is the primary geometry control; show per-camera forest as a breakdown, not a choice. Retain required attribution. Public investor copy stays concise; engineering limitations belong in engineering docs and owner review notes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
English only, including stored preferences and URL parameters. Local IBM Plex, minimum 12 px supporting text, tabular figures, keyboard focus, reduced motion, distinguishable map categories and textual legend. Camera 1/2/3 are always active. Half-open sectors count each target once; vertical limits and LOS still apply. Sector guides use a level local target plane; analysis cells use actual terrain/canopy heights.
