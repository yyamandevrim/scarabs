# ACORN launch point map

## Register
product

## Users
Investors and a hackathon jury experiencing the module, its link to the main centre, and the ranger’s Wi-Fi review. Operators can continue into launch planning.

## Product Purpose
Let visitors handle the real three-camera module in 3D, understand its 360° observation model, see status and smoke reports travel home over LoRa, and review imagery over close-range Wi-Fi. The launch planner compares simulated visible forest over Finnish terrain.

## Brand Personality
Precise, calm, candid. The product is ACORN (Aerial Canopy Observation and Reporting Node); identity and the supplied vector wordmark come only from brand.json, and Scarabs is the team.

## Anti-references
Generic dashboard cards, marketing slogans, decorative badges, illustrative product substitutes, tiny grey figures and ambiguous zeros.

## Design Principles
Let visitors turn the real CAD, follow a human smoke review, then explore the real map. Put the decision beside its evidence. Describe map results as simulated visible forest. Keep licence attribution visible. Separate uncomputed values from measured zero. Use progressive disclosure for secondary controls. The Overview presents the product without a self-critical limits band; owner decisions about removed or uncertain claims are recorded separately.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
English only, including returning visitors with an old language preference. Locally vendored IBM Plex Sans / Mono, readable supporting text, tabular figures, keyboard focus, reduced motion, distinguishable map categories with a textual legend.
